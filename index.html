FAQ

Questions, answered.

Are there really no ads anywhere?

None. Pre-roll, mid-roll, banner, overlays, sponsored shelves and end cards are stripped at build level. There is no "ad-free subscription" — it simply doesn't exist in the app.

What exactly can I customize?

Everything visible: theme colors, fonts, radius, density, player controls and their positions, feed order, hidden shelves, Shorts behaviour, default quality, notification rules and content filters — 120+ switches plus custom theme files.

How does the built-in downloader work?

Tap the download icon on any player, choose 144p–4K video or MP3 audio, and the file is saved to your chosen folder. Playlists and channels can be queued in batch with resumable background downloads.

Does it still have everything from YouTube?

Yes — Shorts, subscriptions, playlists, chapters, live streams, comments, community posts, captions, HDR and 4K playback. You get the entire platform plus controls the official app doesn't expose.

Is my watch history uploaded anywhere?

No. There is no telemetry or analytics SDK. History and downloads stay on your device, and you can use the app without signing in at all.
