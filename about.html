ABOUT THE DEVELOPER

Hey, I'm Praveen.

I built YouTube Modify because I was tired of watching ads before every video, getting sponsored content pushed into my feed, and having zero say in how my own player looks. So I rebuilt the whole thing — and gave every switch to the people using it.

This isn't a company product or an ad-funded wrapper. It's a one-person project made end to end: the interface, the theme engine, the ad removal layer and the external video downloader that sits right inside the player.

Downloader system

THE STORY

Why YouTube Modify exists.

The official app decided what you watch, what you skip and what you look at — ads first, suggestions second, viewer last. I wanted the opposite order.

So the ads were removed at the build level, the feed was handed back to the user, and a full video downloader was placed one tap away from the play button. Nothing is upsell, nothing is locked behind a subscription.
